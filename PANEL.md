# Artist panel: sourcing plan (provisional)

## Who counts as an "artist" here
Working visual artists whose judgement is trained by sustained exposure to images: fine-art photographers, painters, illustrators, art faculty, curators, critics. Require a verifiable portfolio, exhibition record or teaching role. Record each person's discipline and how much of their practice is commercial (stock, advertising, art direction) as a covariate.

## Sources, in order of preference
1. **Direct outreach to Seattle-area art institutions** (teaching artists and faculty). Candidates to approach: Cornish College of the Arts, Photographic Center Northwest, Gage Academy of Art, Pratt Fine Arts Center, and UW School of Art + Art History + Design. Faculty and teaching artists are verifiable, used to giving critique, and a modest honorarium appeals to many of them.
2. **Brad's photography and publishing network** (e.g. through *Digital Negatives* and Focal Press contacts). Highest trust and fastest. Risk: a homogeneous panel of photographers, so balance it with sources 1 and 3.
3. **Freelance platforms with public portfolios** (e.g. Upwork, Behance-linked freelancers). Paid per session, portfolio checkable before hiring. Skews commercial; record that.
4. **Prolific occupation screeners.** Self-reported and unverifiable, so use them only to recruit a larger "art-background" lay subgroup, never as the expert panel.

## Terms
- 6–8 people, one ~90-minute remote session each (or two 45-minute sessions).
- Honorarium ~$75–125 per session, paid regardless of the answers given.
- Written consent covering anonymised use of their ratings and comments. If results will go to a journal, check whether an ethics/IRB review is needed before recruiting.
- Panelists are told the images are AI-generated, and nothing about the hypotheses or arms.
