## The design question: which component sets the attractors?

Attribution uses the persistent atlas: every intermediate state of the four cells pooled, clustered jointly (k = 40), membership within one fixed radius, a cluster "present" in a cell when ≥ 3 visits last ≥ 5 steps. Presence pattern over the 2×2 gives captioner / generator / pair / universal.

**In image space (DINOv2, R 0.20), the generator dominates.**
- A0's 11 persistent attractors: 7 survive the captioner swap (E1-cap: both salons, cathedral, bedroom, bathroom, East Asian garden, dunes), 4 survive the generator swap (E1-gen: cathedral, dancers, bedroom, soccer), 2 survive both swaps (E1-both: dancers, bathroom).
- Attribution of all persistent attractors: pair-specific 13, generator 5, captioner 2 (lone tree in a mirror lake = Qwen; soccer players = LLaVA), three present in all but one cell (cathedral, bedroom: all but E1-both; dancers: all but E1-cap).
- The SDXL cells spend 40% and 38% of their steps inside persistent attractors; the Hunyuan cells 18% and 20%. How attractor-dominated a loop is, is set by the generator.
- E1-cap's endpoints overlap A0 more than E1-gen's do (0.53 vs 0.37 inside A0 radii; Vendi 32 vs 59).

**In caption space (MiniLM, R 0.15), the captioner dominates.**
- A0's 12 persistent caption attractors: 2 survive the captioner swap (E1-cap), 8 survive the generator swap (E1-gen), 1 survives both.
- Attribution: pair-specific 22, captioner 9, generator 0.

**Reading.** An attractor is a joint (image, caption) mode of the pair. Its phrasing is the captioner's and its rendering is the generator's, and each model's share is visible only in its own modality. "Captioner dominates" (H3) is true of the words and false of the pictures; measured where the predictions put it (DINOv2 images), it fails. The motif-level evidence by eye sits between: the cathedral is a LLaVA motif (20 / 15 / 33 / 2 persistent visits across A0 / E1-cap / E1-gen / E1-both; weakest under Qwen) rendered two ways, the mirror-lake tree is a Qwen motif on both generators, the rainbow tunnel and the dancers are Hunyuan motifs with either captioner, and the salons are SDXL motifs with either captioner.

## Gravity and the hazard hypotheses

Escape hazard at one fixed radius, for attractors with ≥ 40 steps in both cells of a pair (`reports/hazard_test.md`): 19 comparisons, median ratio 2.1–2.5 in every pair type, within 2× in 29–44%. The cathedral has hazard 0.024 in A0, 0.076 in E1-cap and 0.204 in E1-gen; the bedroom 0.12 / 0.27 / 0.11. Neither "hazard tracks the attractor" nor "hazard tracks the captioner" holds: depth is a property of the pair's joint mode. Depth and dominance also separate: E1-cap's basins are as occupied as A0's (38% of steps) but include the shallowest (banquet hall 0.078, Fuji 0.048 are deep; salon 0.225 shallow), and the Hunyuan cells' basins are both fewer and shallower in occupancy though some are deep (E1-both vortex 0.07).

## Seed memory

Seed identification (own step-0 image nearer than any other seed's) falls to 50% by step 3–5 and to ~20% by step 10 in all four cells and both spaces, then flattens at 6–12% (seeds that start inside a basin). The forgetting rate is a property of the loop, not the models. After burn-in the process is stationary: step-to-step drift and per-step hazard are flat over steps 20–99.

## Captions (`reports/caption_audit.md`)

- LLaVA ignores the 50-word limit (median 112–131 words), Qwen obeys it (34–37). Hence the clip fires on 97–99% of LLaVA prompts and 1–4% of Qwen's; Hunyuan's mT5 never truncated.
- Style vocabulary rises in every cell from ~50–60% of step-0 captions to 85–96%; photographic vocabulary falls to ≤ 6% (Qwen) or ~25–45% (LLaVA, which uses "realistic" and "snapshot" as stock phrases).
- People: captions mentioning a person fall from 67–79% at step 0 to 14–22% (SDXL cells) but stay at 42–53% in the Hunyuan cells. Gendered words: A0 ~2:1 male throughout; E1-gen ~1:1.2 female; both Qwen cells end 0:39 and 22:318 male:female. The gender prior follows the captioner, the persistence of people follows the generator. Caption counts only; E6 measures images.

## What E1 changes for the programme

1. H3 should be restated for the paper as a two-modality claim, with this report as the evidence; the prediction text stays as written and failed.
2. A0-core-2 (queued) supplies the run-to-run noise floor for every count above.
3. A0-S adds a third captioner so the caption-space result is about captioners, not Qwen vs LLaVA.
4. E2-QZ's control is E1-both; the register-vs-culture question is now sharp.
5. The Hunyuan cells keep people in the loop twice as long as the SDXL cells: E6 should be run on SDXL (planned) and, if budget allows, once on Hunyuan.
