# E3 gate report — text loops (SPEC2 §4 E3 as amended 2026-10-28), 2026-10-29

Six cells, each 20 trajectories (Hintze seeds 0–19) × 100 steps, same model for both steps: E3-1 compress–expand (story → 50-word summary → story) and E3-2 rewrite ("Rewrite this story, same length") under claude-sonnet-5-5 (default sampling, D16, effort low), qwen2.5vl:7b text-only (T 0.9) and llava:latest 7B text-only (T 0.9). Each E3-2 cell starts from its E3-1 sibling's step-0 stories (byte-identical). Predictions: `predictions.md` "E3 text loops" (written 2026-10-28 before any run; not edited since). Analysis: `analysis/e3_report.py` → `reports/e3.json`, `reports/e3/tables.md`, `reports/e3/curves.png`, `reports/e3/cluster_<k>.md` (text contact sheets). Runs: `runs/e3_{1,2}_{sonnet,qwen,llava}`.

**Gate: all six cells are complete and clean (12,000 records, one start per cell, no crash, no resume; 7B cells 17 h on the laptop, Sonnet cells 1.9 h and $54.02 over the API). The headline result is the opposite of the prediction's premise: text loops do not converge across trajectories. At the caption-space radius (0.15) no persistent attractor is shared by more than one trajectory in any cell, Vendi over the 20 trajectories is flat from step 1 to step 99, and the nearest other trajectory is 0.37–0.56 away where the image cells' captions sit 0.15–0.19 apart at the same n. Each trajectory digs its own well: locally the loops are as sticky as the image loops (consecutive steps 0.12–0.20 apart, 5-step windows cohesive 82–87% of the time), and the rewrite loop freezes almost entirely (consecutive 0.03–0.05, 88–96% of late steps within 0.15 of the trajectory's own centroid). The seed is remembered 3–10× longer than in the image loops. The bottleneck matters: E3-1 drifts 0.45–0.64 from its step-1 story by step 99, E3-2 only 0.27–0.41, and under the 7B models E3-2 shrinks to ~90 words despite "same length". Brad's branch of prediction 2 (bottleneck is the cause of attraction) holds under the decision rule; prediction 1's "same model, different scale" does not: the shared-attractor model does not transfer to text at this scale and in this space. What the text loops do show is the model's default *themes*, which are visible in the summaries and the name census but not as proximity in MiniLM space.**

**Is the missing atlas an artefact of the space or of n = 20?** No, on three controls (`e3/tables.md` "Scale controls").
- *Local scale is the same.* Consecutive-step distance in story space (E3-1: 0.12–0.20) matches caption space (A0-S 0.15, E1-cap 0.20); the share of 5-step windows cohesive at 0.15 is 82–87% for E3-1 and 69–89% for the caption cells. The radius is not too small for stories.
- *Between trajectories the spaces differ.* The nearest step of any other trajectory (steps ≥ 25) is a median 0.37–0.56 away in every text cell, and never within 0.15 (0.2% of steps in E3-1 Qwen, 0 elsewhere). The caption cells subsampled to 20 trajectories (20 draws) give 0.15–0.19, with 36–51% of steps within 0.15 of another trajectory. Image-loop captions meet each other; stories never do.
- *The summaries say the same.* MiniLM truncates at 256 word pieces, so a Sonnet story (807 pieces) is embedded by its opening third. The E3-1 summaries (60 pieces, fully covered, the direct caption analog) give the same answer: nearest other trajectory 0.44–0.52, no step within 0.15, Vendi at 99 flat.
- *Half-life at n = 20.* Caption cells subsampled to 20 trajectories have a half-life of 6 (range 3–12); the text cells' 14–45 and > 99 are outside that range.

**Each trajectory in its own well.** Share of steps 50–99 within 0.15 of the trajectory's own late centroid: E3-1 0.49–0.58 (caption cells 0.55–0.74), E3-2 0.88–0.96. The E3-2 "attractors" the joint atlas reports are exactly these: one trajectory each (e.g. E3-2 Qwen trajectory 4 sitting 79 steps in a "Lily by the stream" story, trajectory 15 in "Dr. Elara Voss, Paris, 22nd century"). The rewrite loop is a copy machine with occasional jumps (276 and 162 verbatim copies under Qwen and LLaVA, 2 under Sonnet) and, under the 7B models, a shrinking one: median length 441 → 306 words at step 1 → 89 (Qwen) and 75 (LLaVA) at step 99, the bottleneck creeping into the loop that was built without one. Sonnet holds 530 ± 10 words throughout.

## Deviations and caveats
- **D16** applies: Sonnet ran at the API default sampling (temperature rejected), the 7B models at T 0.9. Prediction 8's flag did not fire.
- **Refusal flag is unreliable on stories.** `REFUSAL_RE` (written for captions) fired on dialogue: 557 Sonnet E3-1 stories ("I'm sorry" 351, "I can't" 195), 200 in E3-2, 8 Qwen, 15 LLaVA. Every sampled hit is a character speaking; all Sonnet calls ended `end_turn`. The flag is kept in the records as logged; the report does not use it.
- **Word counts of Chinese stories** (Qwen, 78 + 169 records) are whitespace counts and wrong; they are excluded from the length and audit statistics, and counted via the `cjk` flag.
- **MiniLM covers 256 word pieces**: a Sonnet story is embedded by its opening ~180 words. The summary space (fully covered) is reported alongside and agrees on the headline.
- **Lead-name heuristic** (most frequent capitalised non-initial token whose lower-case form is rare) lets a few titles and places through (Secretary, Latin, Willowbrook, Trafalgar); the top-10 tables are read with that in mind.
- `vaderSentiment 3.3.2` and `textstat 0.7.10` added to `requirements.txt` for the audit.

## Reading
1. **The shared-attractor model is a property of the image loop, not of looping.** With the renderer and the caption bottleneck gone, nothing pushes different stories toward each other. The image loop's gravity came from the pair: a generator that renders many captions into the same kind of scene, and a captioner that describes many scenes with the same words. A single language model rewriting its own text keeps each story in its own neighbourhood.
2. **The bottleneck is what moves a trajectory.** Compress–expand drifts two to three times farther than rewrite and forgets its seed; rewrite freezes. Brad's first branch of prediction 2 holds in all three models. The alternate (summary ↔ story bouncing between two attractors) does not: the summaries do not converge either.
3. **Defaults show at the theme and name level**, which is what Brad's framing asked for ("the default themes trained into the model"): Qwen's community-sustainability uplift and Sarah, LLaVA's success stories and Sarah/Jack, Sonnet's grief-and-reconciliation with Mara and Marguerite. MiniLM proximity is the wrong instrument for that level; the next measurement should code themes directly (a content-word or archetype coding, or a judge under rule 4's constraints) rather than cluster embeddings.
4. **The 7B rewrite loops collapse in length**, not in topic. "Same length" is not obeyed; ~90 words is where both settle. That is the E4 bottleneck-length question answered for free in one direction: a long bottleneck without a renderer does not create shared attractors either.
