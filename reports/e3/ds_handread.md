## Calls made before the judge

- Hand-read overlap with Haiku's five: **3 of Sonnet's top five** (S1 ↔ H3, S2 ↔ H1/H5, S4 ↔ H2). DS1's hand-read clause (≥ 2) holds on this read.
- Expected moral-judge fit: S1, S2 and S4 together are ≈ 0.65 of late sentences, so a fit ≥ 0.5 is expected, with H3 the top moral and H1/H5 splitting S2.
- DS4 (the confession-and-forgiveness moral as the top moral the judge scores "none"): on this read the morals outside Haiku's five are
  S3 (accepting help) and S5 (unseen care), not confession; the apology/confession sentences (S4) mostly fit H2. I expect DS4 to fail:
  the "none" stories will be mostly S3 and S5.
- Within-family shape: every Sonnet moral is domestic and relational (grief, kindness, help, estrangement); none is civic. This is Haiku's
  menu with two additions (S3, S5) and a stronger emphasis on the giver's return and on presence over words.
