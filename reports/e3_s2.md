## R4 — seeds do not pick wells: fails (seeds leave a trace)

| clause | predicted | measured |
|---|---|---|
| same-seed pairs (trajectory t in A and in B) sharing a persistent joint well | ≤ 3 of 100 | **9 of 100** (0.54 expected under independence at the pooled rate) |
| same-seed minus different-seed median late nearest-step distance | < 0.02 | **−0.041** (same-seed pairs are closer: 0.330 vs 0.371) |
| cross-sample rate vs within-sample rate | within 2 × | 0.0056 vs 0.0052 (holds) |

The nine same-seed pairs are not generic: seed 14 (fog, the old dock) gives in both samples a note asking her to meet at the water after
his boat sank; seed 75 (a small object found near a child) gives in both a warm stone humming the mother's lullaby by a dried creek;
seed 71 (the store's dwindling stock) gives Haldane Hardware and its ledger in both; seed 77 (lacing up dancing shoes) gives the performer
who freezes; seed 70 (the national team's cracks) the grieving teammate; seed 91 (Willow Creek's governance) the widower and the
neighbour. So a seed's content biases which well the trajectory reaches, long after the seed itself is unidentifiable (own-seed
identification in the 100-seed cells is in the cell table below), and the same seed can produce a *seed-specific* well in two independent
runs. The cross-vs-within rate holding means this seed effect is not what makes the wells: shared wells form between different seeds at the
same rate as between repeats of a seed; the seed only tilts the choice among them. The pooled atlas therefore slightly over-counts
model wells when seeds repeat: 9 of its 51 shared wells have a same-seed pair in them.

## What this gate changes

1. The E3-S claims survive an independent sample: constant per-pair rate, many small wells, wells shared across samples as families.
2. The matching method matters at the margin: joint refit 6 of 13, fixed-reference projection 7 of 13. Fixed-reference projection is the

3. Seeds are not inert: 9 of 100 same-seed pairs share a well against 0.5 expected. Future cells should either use fresh seeds per sample
   or report the same-seed pairs separately, and the "attractors are the model's" sentence in the article needs the qualifier "the seed
   tilts the choice among them".

   first collection") is met on both halves: 7 of 13 recur, 4 new wells, 3 of them touched by the first sample.
